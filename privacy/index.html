Last updated 31 October 2025. Based on Al Khair's privacy policy on thehoney4u.com.

Who we are

Al Khair Honey (PTY) LTD, 86 The Drive, Lakefield, Benoni, Gauteng, South Africa. Privacy requests: sales@thehoney4u.com or 081 273 2000.

What we collect

  • Identity and contact details: name, email and phone.
  • Billing and delivery addresses.
  • Order details: products, notes and invoices.
  • Support questions and other messages you send.
  • Technical data: IP address, device, operating system and browser.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, time on page, referring links and error logs.
  • Approximate location (city or region), worked out from your IP address.
  • From partners: payment status from the payment gateway, delivery events from couriers and aggregated analytics.

Card details

Payments are handled by the payment gateway. Al Khair does not store full card numbers or CVV codes.

Why we use it

  • To process, fulfil and deliver orders.
  • To manage accounts, order history and support requests.
  • To send messages about your orders and deliveries.
  • To improve the website, prevent fraud and keep it secure.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • For marketing, with your consent or as the law allows. You can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

Al Khair does not sell personal information. It is shared only where needed:

  • Payment processors, to complete transactions.
  • Couriers and logistics partners, to deliver orders and provide tracking.
  • IT and analytics providers, to host, secure and analyse the website.
  • Professional advisers and authorities, where the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if the business is merged, acquired or sold.

Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies run the site, remember your preferences and help us understand how it's used. They fall into four groups: strictly necessary, preferences, analytics and marketing. Marketing cookies are only used with your consent. You can control cookies in your browser; blocking some may stop parts of the site working.

This preview site also keeps your basket, wishlist and demo orders in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to Al Khair.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purposes above. Orders and invoices are kept for the statutory tax and accounting periods; support records for service quality and resolving disputes; analytics in aggregate or for short operational periods.

Your rights

Al Khair may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

  • Ask to see the personal information Al Khair holds about you.
  • Ask for it to be corrected or deleted, where the law allows.
  • Object to, or limit, certain processing.
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): complaints.IR@justice.gov.za, inforegulator.org.za.

Children

The website is meant for people aged 18 and over. Al Khair does not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

The site uses HTTPS encryption, access controls and secure hosting. No transmission or storage is completely secure, but Al Khair takes reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

Updates take effect when they are posted on this page.
