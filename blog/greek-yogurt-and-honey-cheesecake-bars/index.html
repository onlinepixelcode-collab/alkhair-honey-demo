Greek Yogurt and Honey Cheesecake Bars

Step by step

Method

Tap a step when it’s done.

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (fan-assisted) or 190°C (conventional). Line an 18x18cm baking dish with parchment paper.

  2. In a medium bowl, whisk until smooth

    • 240g Greek yogurt
    • 120ml organic honey
    • 60g granulated sugar
    • 1 tsp vanilla extract
    • 1/4 tsp salt

  3. Prepare the crust

    • Mix 120g graham cracker crumbs and 30g melted unsalted butter until combined.
    • Press mixture into the prepared baking dish.

  4. Layer the cheesecake mixture over the crust.

  5. Sprinkle 60g chopped walnuts on top.

  6. Bake for 35-40 minutes or until edges are set and center is slightly jiggly.

  7. Cool completely in the baking dish.

  8. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.

  9. Cut into bars and serve chilled.

Recipe from Al Khair, first published on thehoney4u.com.

From Al Khair’s kitchen

Tips

  • Use high-quality Greek yogurt for the best texture.
  • Adjust honey to taste for desired sweetness.
  • Add lemon zest or juice for extra flavor.
